Digital Photo and Document Recovery

Water-damaged documents go through a controlled drying process that prevents mold and further deterioration. If your Buena Park documents were saturated by firefighting water, professionals do not simply air-dry them. Instead, they use vacuum freeze-drying, a technique where documents are frozen and then slowly dried under vacuum. This method preserves the texture and appearance of the paper while removing all moisture. The process takes weeks but produces significantly better results than standard drying methods.

Lamination and reinforcement are options for documents that suffered severe damage. If paper is torn or brittle, professionals can gently reinforce the back side with archival materials, making the document sturdy enough to handle and store. Lamination seals the document and prevents future moisture damage. For truly irreplaceable items like old family documents or original certificates, this reinforcement extends their lifespan for decades.

If you find documents or photos after a fire, stop and think before touching them.
