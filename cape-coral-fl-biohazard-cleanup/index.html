HEPA stands for High-Efficiency Particulate Air and represents the gold standard in air filtration technology. HEPA filters capture particles as small as 0. 3 microns, which includes most bacteria and viruses. The filters work through multiple mechanisms including interception, impaction, and diffusion to trap particles. Multiple layers in HEPA filters create a progressively finer filtration network. A true HEPA filter removes 99. 97 percent of particles passing through it during laboratory testing. In real-world applications, HEPA filtration still removes the vast majority of airborne contaminants. Cape Coral cleanup professionals use HEPA filters in portable air scrubbers and fixed filtration systems. Portable HEPA units can be placed in specific rooms or moved throughout the property. HEPA filtration is particularly effective against particles generated during biohazard cleanup and remediation work. HEPA certification requires meeting strict performance standards.
