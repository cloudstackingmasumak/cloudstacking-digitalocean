Professional Boarding and Fastening Techniques

Boarding up a fire-damaged Edmonds home is not simple plywood placement. It requires structural knowledge, proper fastening methods, and materials rated for permanence. Our crews assess the integrity of surrounding structures before fastening boards. We verify which studs and headers remain load-bearing. We account for settling that might occur as the structure cools or as water dries out. We use fastening systems that hold securely through wind gusts without causing additional structural damage.

Plywood is selected based on the opening size, anticipated wind loads, and local building code requirements. We use weather-resistant materials and seal all seams properly to prevent water infiltration at the board edges. Fastening includes not just nails but also metal brackets and reinforcement where needed. We overlap seams correctly so water cannot find gaps. We ensure that boards are angled slightly to shed water away from the structure. This professional approach ensures that temporary boards remain secure through Edmonds' rainy season and wind patterns. Improper boarding actually causes additional damage as water infiltrates around poorly sealed edges, creating a false sense of security while moisture damages hidden interior areas. Our method prevents this hidden damage scenario entirely.
