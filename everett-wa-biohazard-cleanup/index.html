Worker Decontamination Procedures

Professional teams in Everett change into clean clothes before leaving your property. Contaminated clothing goes into biohazard bags for proper cleaning or disposal. This meticulous attention to decontamination prevents workers from carrying pathogens to their families or other clients in Everett.

Personal protective equipment protects workers, but containment engineering protects the rest of the building and surrounding areas. Professionals establish physical barriers using heavy-duty plastic sheeting sealed with tape around doorways, vents, and any openings between the contaminated zone and unaffected areas. These barriers extend from floor to ceiling and are sealed with duct tape to prevent any gaps. Professionals then operate negative air pressure equipment that removes air from the contained space at a faster rate than air can leak in, creating a slight vacuum that directs any escaping air through HEPA filtration rather than into adjacent spaces. This negative pressure is maintained throughout the remediation work and continues for a period after work completion. Pressure gauges monitor the containment continuously, alerting crews to any breaches that would compromise the system.
