Beyond the structure itself, fire destroys the contents inside your Fort Myers home: furniture, clothing, electronics, kitchen equipment, books, and family heirlooms. Many of these items can be rescued and restored if handled properly and quickly, but every hour of delay worsens the outcome. Soot settles onto every surface, water from firefighting saturates fabrics and wood, and heat warps plastic and metal. Attempting to clean soot-covered belongings with household products typically fails and can spread contamination to unaffected items. Professional pack-out services remove contents from the affected areas, transport them to secure facilities, sort them by restoration potential, and apply appropriate cleaning or restoration techniques. Understanding the pack-out process and what items can realistically be saved helps you prepare for recovery and know what decisions you will face.

Once fire damage is assessed, restoration professionals begin using industrial-strength chemicals to eliminate mold, bacteria, and odor-causing compounds that thrive in damaged materials. These chemicals are essential for proper remediation. They are not, however, safe for young children or household pets to inhale or contact through their skin.

Enzyme-based deodorizers, phenolic disinfectants, and quaternary ammonium compounds are all commonly used in fire damage cleanup throughout Fort Myers. These substances work by denaturing proteins and disrupting microbial cell membranes. In the process, they release fumes that are mildly irritating to adult humans and significantly more hazardous to children and pets. A child who wanders through an area where these chemicals were recently applied can absorb them through inhalation or skin contact, developing headaches, nausea, or respiratory irritation within hours.
