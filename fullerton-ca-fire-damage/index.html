Ozone Treatment and Its Proper Application

Ozone treatment is another powerful tool for eliminating fire odor from homes in Fullerton. Ozone is a highly reactive form of oxygen that breaks down odor molecules at a molecular level. When ozone gas circulates through a home, it oxidizes the compounds responsible for persistent smoke smell, converting them into odorless substances.

The ozone process requires careful execution and professional expertise. Homes must be evacuated and sealed tightly during treatment because ozone can irritate lungs and eyes in high concentrations. Professional crews follows strict protocols to ensure the ozone generator runs for the appropriate time at the correct concentration level for the size of your Fullerton home. The process typically takes several hours, and the home must air out completely before reoccupancy.

Complete deodorization is rarely accomplished in a single day of work. Most properties in Fullerton require multiple coordinated treatments over one to two weeks for comprehensive results. Initial thermal fogging or ozone treatment addresses the bulk of offensive odor quickly. Activated charcoal and enzymatic treatments continue working in the background during the following days. Open windows and running ventilation systems helps dissipate remaining odors throughout the recovery period. Follow-up inspections ensure that progress is being made toward complete odor elimination.
