Body Coverage and Protective Suits

Biohazard cleanup requires full body coverage to prevent contaminated materials from contacting skin, hair, or personal clothing. Professional teams' crews wear disposable full-body protective suits made from materials that repel liquids and prevent penetration of blood, body fluids, and other biohazard materials. Each crew member also wears double gloves, with an inner layer of latex or nitrile and an outer layer rated for heavy-duty chemical and biological hazard protection.
