Warranty and Service Terms

Never hire a contractor based on verbal estimates. Get detailed written estimates that specify exactly what work will be performed, which materials will be used, and what the costs are. The estimate should break down labor, materials, and any other charges clearly. Review contract terms carefully before signing. Make sure it specifies the scope of work, timeline, payment terms, and warranties on completed work. Some contractors offer partial payment options or financing to help manage costs. Be cautious of contracts with vague language or terms you don't understand. A legitimate contractor explains everything clearly and doesn't pressure you to sign immediately. Compare estimates from multiple contractors before deciding, price matters, but the lowest bid isn't always the best value.
