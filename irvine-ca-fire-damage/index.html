Odor Removal Strategies and Approaches

Smoke odor persists because VOCs bind to surfaces throughout your home and continue off-gassing odor for extended periods. Removing this odor requires multiple approaches working simultaneously. Professional HVAC cleaning removes odor sources from the air distribution system. Thorough surface cleaning removes soot that contains odor molecules. Professional ozone treatment at high concentrations breaks down VOC molecules chemically, neutralizing odor at the molecular level. This treatment requires vacating the home for several hours and venting the ozone afterward, but it can effectively eliminate residual odors. Activated charcoal placed throughout your home absorbs odor molecules from the air. Leaving windows open to provide air circulation and ventilation helps dissipate remaining odors, though in Irvine's climate, opening windows to hot, humid outdoor air is impractical for extended periods. Baking soda sprinkled on carpets and furniture absorbs odor and can be vacuumed up after several hours.
