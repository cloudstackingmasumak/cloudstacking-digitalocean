Restoration vs. Remediation

There's an important distinction between restoration and remediation in biohazard cleanup. Remediation focuses on removing the hazard and making a space minimally safe. Restoration goes further, returning the space to its original condition as completely as possible. We believe in restoration because we know that's what families in Lafayette actually need. Thorough restoration means cleaning every surface, removing all contamination, and addressing the deeper impact of what happened.
