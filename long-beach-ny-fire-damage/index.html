Why Immediate Boarding Is Critical

Water damage compounds faster than almost any other post-fire threat. A broken window in Long Beach means rain and monsoon-season moisture pour directly onto interior walls, insulation, and flooring. Within hours, drywall begins absorbing water, and within days, mold spores germinate in the damp. Water also wicks into framing lumber, creating soft spots that weaken structural integrity and require replacement rather than repair. Firefighting water that could not escape through normal drainage routes pools in closed rooms, and open windows allow fresh water to join what remains. Professional board-up crews install weathertight barriers that shed rain and reduce interior moisture immediately. Tarping over roof damage, sealing broken windows, and closing door frames stops the progression of this secondary damage.
