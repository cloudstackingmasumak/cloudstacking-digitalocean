Pathogenic Organisms in Sewage

Hepatitis A from sewage contamination can cause severe liver inflammation, lasting illness, and in severe cases, liver failure requiring hospitalization. Cryptosporidium diarrhea can persist for weeks and becomes life-threatening in individuals with immune system compromise. Legionella from sewage aerosols causes legionnaires disease, a serious pneumonia that requires antibiotic treatment. These are not rare complications; they occur regularly in families who delayed professional cleanup or attempted inadequate DIY remediation.
