Insulation Assessment and Replacement

Insulation in your O Fallon home may be damaged by biohazard incidents or by the water used in cleanup. Insulation that has been wet must be removed and replaced because it loses its insulating properties when wet and becomes a breeding ground for mold. Even insulation that is not visibly damaged should be assessed for contamination.

Attic insulation requires special attention because it is often forgotten during cleanup. If any biohazard material has reached the attic, the insulation must be removed and replaced. Contamination in attic insulation is particularly serious because it affects the air quality of your entire O Fallon home as the heating and cooling system draws air through the attic space.
