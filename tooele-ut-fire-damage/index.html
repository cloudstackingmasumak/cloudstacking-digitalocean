Smoke travels from the garage into adjacent walls, up through attics, and into living spaces, depositing soot and odor-causing particles throughout the entire home. In Tooele, a one-car garage attached directly to a house creates a direct pathway for contamination into every room, from bedrooms to kitchens.

Concrete Floor Assessment and Restoration

Concrete exposed to intense heat can be damaged. Spalling (surface flaking), discoloration, and reduced structural integrity are common. Thermal shock from firefighting water can cause concrete to crack.

Minor heat damage, surface blackening or light discoloration, can be cleaned and sealed. The concrete remains functional.

Severe damage, deep spalls, large cracks, or structural compromise, requires repair or replacement. A contractor assesses whether patching is sufficient or whether the entire slab must be replaced.

Replacing a garage concrete floor costs 2,000 to 5,000 dollars depending on size. Patching and sealing costs 500 to 1,500 dollars. Your contractor in Tooele will assess which approach suits your situation.
