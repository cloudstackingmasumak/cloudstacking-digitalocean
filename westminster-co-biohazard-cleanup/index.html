Liability Insurance and Accident Coverage

If the biohazard situation resulted from an accident for which someone else is liable, their liability insurance might cover your cleanup costs. For example, if someone else's negligence caused an injury requiring biohazard cleanup, their insurance company should cover remediation. This applies in Westminster, Colorado just as anywhere else.

Contact your attorney or the liable party's insurance company to explore this possibility. Document the circumstances of the incident thoroughly to support any insurance claim. Gathering photos, witness statements, and medical records creates the evidence base for an insurance claim.
